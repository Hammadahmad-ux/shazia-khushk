# Image Quality Audit

## Severity Definitions

- **CRITICAL:** must not be published; product/brand/text accuracy or visible generation artifacts could misrepresent what the customer receives.
- **HIGH:** not production-ready; requires replacement, authoritative comparison, rights/claim approval, or a controlled reshoot.
- **MEDIUM:** usable only as an internal direction/reference asset; production derivative/source deliverables are missing.
- **LOW:** minor correction that does not affect product truth. No supplied image qualifies as only LOW.

High pixel dimensions do not override inaccurate packaging or generated content. Do not “fix” label text by retouching a false render; replace it with media made from the verified physical product or approved source artwork.
