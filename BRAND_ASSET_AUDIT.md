# Brand Asset Audit

## Detected Brand Name and Architecture

The clearest official-looking logo asset reads **SHAZIA KHUSHK**, with the tagline **TRADITIONAL SINDHI & BALOCHI WEAR**. Product packaging repeatedly uses **SKZI**, sometimes with “Shazia Khushk” beneath it. The most defensible current interpretation is:

- **Store/master brand:** Shazia Khushk.
- **Product mark:** SKZI.
- **Unreliable wording:** “MANAJ BY SHAZIA KHUSHK” appears only in probable AI clothing creatives and conflicts with the logo; do not publish it.

Client confirmation is still required on whether SKZI is an endorsed product line, an abbreviation of the master brand, or the intended public store name across all categories.

## Logo Structure

- **Format supplied:** one 1254×1254 JPEG; no vector or transparent version.
- **Lockup:** high-contrast serif uppercase wordmark, widely tracked; thin horizontal rules and a small floral divider beneath; smaller uppercase tagline.
- **Orientation:** the actual wordmark is horizontal, but it sits low within a square canvas with substantial empty space.
- **Symbol/icon:** the divider flower is decorative; no approved standalone symbol is supplied in the logo folder. An `SK` monogram appears in collateral mockups, but is not established as an approved logo asset.
- **Proportions:** wordmark is wide and shallow; the supplied square canvas is unsuitable for a compact site header without an approved alternate lockup.
- **Transparency:** none. The oxblood texture is baked into the JPEG.
- **Light-background use:** not available as a transparent mark. The JPEG would appear as a large dark square.
- **Dark-background use:** visually compatible only as the complete opaque asset on a matching dark composition; it cannot safely be extracted or recolored.
- **Legibility:** the main name is clear at moderate size; the thin tagline and wide tracking fail quickly at small sizes.
- **Provisional minimum:** the complete supplied square needs roughly 260–300 CSS px width for its tagline to remain reasonably legible. This makes it inappropriate for normal header use. Request proper horizontal and compact exports rather than cropping this original.

## Photography and Asset Characteristics

### Clothing

- Most stills are probable AI-generated multi-look boards using near-identical models, warm garden backgrounds, ornate garments, and baked promotional text.
- They suggest a feminine, traditional, occasion-wear direction with muted pastels, deep jewel colors, embroidery, beads, sequins and regional craft cues.
- They do not prove physical garment construction, fit, texture, color accuracy, or product boundaries.
- The only clearly live-action clothing asset is a low-resolution vertical saree video with mixed environments and an unverified retail-opening overlay.

### Fragrance and care

- ChatGPT-named images use clean portrait compositions, stone/marble surfaces, soft botanicals, warm or cool directional light, centered bottles and generous negative space.
- These renders are visually coherent in isolation but do not share one verified packaging system. Logos, bottle colors, closures, labels and product names vary.
- Real phone photography exists for the shampoo and is the strongest physical-product evidence, but is not a controlled e-commerce shoot.
- Several staged WhatsApp images are probable AI composites with corrupted labels, unrealistic repetition, or unverified ingredient/benefit text.

### Brand/editorial

- Root creatives establish oxblood, warm cream, muted gold, fine rules, floral/folk ornament and high-contrast serif typography.
- Brand-story creatives use traditional dress, biography, heritage and craftsmanship language. Rights and factual approval are required before reuse.
- Nearly all supplied editorial copy is baked into images and therefore unsuitable for responsive, searchable, accessible page content.

## Visual Personality Supported by Real Evidence

The strongest defensible direction is **heritage editorial with contemporary commerce discipline**:

- culturally rooted, formal and expressive without stereotyped decoration;
- warm, craft-led and ceremonial for clothing;
- clean, clear and product-specific for fragrance and personal care;
- premium through typography, material detail and spacing rather than glass effects, gradients or heavy ornament;
- anchored by oxblood/cream brand heritage but flexible enough that blue, green, amber and pink packaging can remain accurate.

UI UX Pro Max surfaced editorial grid and Swiss grid discipline as useful structural patterns. Its automatic Liquid Glass, accent-pink, oversized minimalism, 3D and texture-heavy suggestions are rejected: they conflict with the actual identity, accessibility, mobile performance and the requirement to let products remain legible.

## Brand and Product Inconsistencies

- `SHAZIA KHUSHK`, `SKZI`, `SKZI SHAZIA KHUSHK`, `MANAJ BY SHAZIA KHUSHK`, and an `SK` monogram appear without a confirmed hierarchy.
- The logo tagline limits the brand to traditional clothing while the catalog includes fragrance, skincare and hair care.
- Lotus/floral marks differ across logo, shampoo, fragrance, serum and oil packaging.
- Serum identities use at least three visual systems: `SKZI / SKIN | SCIENCE | SOUL`, plain lotus/wordmark renders, and shampoo-style circular SKZI branding.
- The same Hair Growth Serum appears in white and green bottles with different label architecture.
- Clothing boards show the same color names with different garments/embroidery; they cannot be treated as a stable variant system.
- Root collateral uses refined oxblood/cream craft cues, while personal-care renders shift among clinical white/blue, botanical green and gold luxury scenes.

## Accessibility Concerns

- Thin gold display type and small widely tracked taglines will lose legibility; gold-on-cream must not be used for body text.
- Baked text cannot reflow, scale, translate, or receive proper semantic structure; most promotional creatives fail mobile readability.
- Busy garment collages do not support concise alt text or product comparison.
- Dark textured surfaces complicate contrast and focus-ring consistency.
- Color labels must remain text, not swatch-only controls; actual garment color cannot be trusted from generated images.
- Informative video needs a client-approved poster, captions/transcript and controls; the supplied file has none.

## Recommended Visual Direction

1. **Use a light-led storefront:** warm cream or near-neutral commerce surfaces allow varied products to remain accurate. Use deep oxblood for key brand bands, editorial moments and strong text—not every page background.
2. **Treat gold as ornament, not UI text:** use it in approved logo artwork, dividers or large decorative details; use a solid high-contrast text/accent color for buttons, links and focus states.
3. **Separate expression from commerce:** heritage/editorial modules may carry craft motifs and asymmetry; product grids, PDP controls, cart and checkout remain clear, calm and grid-led.
4. **Create two connected shoot modes:** consistent isolated product photography for comparison and controlled editorial photography for category storytelling. Do not mix AI package renders with real photos in one gallery.
5. **Let clothing lead the master-brand story only if the client confirms it:** the tagline and heritage materials support clothing most strongly. Fragrance and care need an approved endorsed-brand relationship and more restrained product-specific presentation.
6. **Do not select final fonts yet:** the logo suggests a high-contrast serif display voice, but body/UI typography, Urdu coverage, licensing and compatibility with the wordmark remain to be tested.

- Horizontal wordmark, compact mark, monochrome, light-on-dark and dark-on-light variants.
- Clear-space, minimum-size, background and recoloring rules.

- Approved brand story, founder portrait/source rights and claim evidence.
- Final packaging artwork or physical-product reference for every fragrance/care product.
