# Merchandising Map

## Production Readiness Rule

No supplied asset is an unconditional homepage hero or complete PDP gallery. Rankings identify the **best conditional candidates**, not publication approvals. An asset remains blocked until its product identity, packaging, claims, rights and client approval match the physical item. Weak assets must not be forced into `PAGE_BLUEPRINTS.md` merely to fill a section.

## Recommended Storefront Taxonomy

### Header navigation

1. **Shop** — all confirmed products, curated rather than inventory-dense.
2. **Clothing** — traditional women’s wear.
3. **Fragrance** — eau de parfum products.
4. **Beauty & Hair Care** — skin serums and hair-care products.

### Internal collection structure

- **Clothing**
  - Short Shirts & Suits — wording pending exact included pieces.
  - Kaftans — pending real product mapping.
  - Sarees — only if the video garment is a launch product.
- **Fragrance**
  - Eau de Parfum.
- **Beauty & Hair Care**
  - Skin Serums: Eye Serum, Niacinamide, Anti Aging, Alpha.
  - Hair Care: Hair Growth Serum, Herbal Protein Shampoo, Hair Oil.

Do not expose Serum, Shampoo and Oil as three separate top-level navigation items. With the detected catalog, that would fragment a small range and give single-product collections disproportionate weight. `Featured`, `New`, `Heritage` and seasonal stories should be curated collections, not taxonomy.

- Zorvan, Delina and individual serum renders have useful portrait composition, but remain conditional; never publish them merely because they fit 4:5.
- The root brand-introduction and all clothing boards contain baked small text and should not be shown as scaled mobile images.
- The saree video is naturally vertical but too low-resolution and commercially ambiguous for a sticky/full-screen hero.
- Category discovery on mobile must use future single-subject images, not dense montages.
- Product cards should fall back to one accurate primary image rather than crossfading to a conflicting or unlabeled render.
